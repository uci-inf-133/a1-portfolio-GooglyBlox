About

I'm a junior in Informatics at UC Irvine and part of the Campuswide Honors Collegium. I like building things for the web and figuring out how to make software that people actually want to use.

I've been doing full-stack development for about eight years now, mostly with Next.js, TypeScript, and Node. These days I split my time between school, freelance work, and a couple of startups I'm building with friends.

When I'm not working on something with a deadline, I'm usually tinkering with browser extensions, userscripts, or whatever small tool I need that doesn't exist yet. I like solving problems that bug me personally and sometimes other people find them useful too.
